# Legend of Arlo: The Guardian's Gauntlet

A Legend of Zelda-inspired browser game: Arlo must navigate the Gauntlet's
puzzles and foes to reach the Master Sword, then plan an escape past
enemies who detect him back to an end shrine.

## Stack
- JavaScript and Three.js
- Vite dev server

## Status
playable · arrow keys to move, retrieve the sword, then escape the Gauntlet
